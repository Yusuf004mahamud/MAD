Lesson 6 Practical - Tenant Management Screen (final version, Parts A-G)

Package used: com.example.tenantmanagementsystemgroupa
If your project's package is different, change:
  1. the "package" line at the top of MainActivity.kt and Tenant.kt
  2. the databinding import in MainActivity.kt
  3. the <variable type="..."> in activity_main.xml

How to use:
  1. Copy the three files into your Android Studio project:
       app/src/main/java/<your package>/MainActivity.kt
       app/src/main/java/<your package>/Tenant.kt
       app/src/main/res/layout/activity_main.xml
  2. Enable view + data binding (see build.gradle.kts.snippet.txt) and Sync.
  3. Build > Rebuild Project, then Run.
